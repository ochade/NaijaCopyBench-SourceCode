Q: Under Nigerian law, a Nigerian archive made preservation copies of works in its collection. May it lend those copies to users?
GOLD (Nigerian): a Nigerian archive can lend preservation copies of works in its collection to users, for the law allows archives make to and distribute copies of works protected under the Copyright Act as part of their ordinary activities, provides its for non-commercial purposes.
US: 17 USC 108(b)(2) and (c)(2) EXPRESSLY forbid making digital copies available outside the library premises. Nigeria s.25(2)(a) permits copies made under the exception to be LENT TO USERS.
UK: Library lending is governed by the public lending right and licensing rather than a general exception.

GOLD (Nigerian): a person cannot ask a platform for the name of a person violating his copyright rights in order to sue them. instead they are to apply to the court for an order to a service provider to identify the alleged infringer
